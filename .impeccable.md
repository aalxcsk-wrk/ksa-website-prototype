# .impeccable.md

## Design Context

### Users
**Primary**: Facilities managers, process engineers, and procurement managers at semiconductor fabs, pharmaceutical cleanrooms, F&B processing plants, industrial operations, and commercial facilities across Singapore, Malaysia, and the broader Southeast Asia region. These are mid-to-senior technical professionals making considered B2B purchasing decisions — often with procurement cycles measured in months.

**Secondary**: General facilities/operations managers at non-semiconductor industrial facilities (hospitality, commercial, government), who encounter the energy management or heating solutions verticals.

**Context of use**: Desktop-first, during working hours at an office or fab facility. High technical literacy. Not browsing casually — evaluating specific solutions or qualifying a vendor. They need to rapidly confirm that Kromax South Asia is credible, experienced, and offers what they need.

**Job to be done**: Assess whether KSA is a credible supplier for a specific solution category → download a brochure or make first contact.

### Brand Personality
**Three words**: Precise. Trusted. Regional.

Kromax South Asia (KSA) is not a global brand trying to appear local — they are a regional specialist with 22+ years embedded in Southeast Asia's semiconductor and industrial sectors. They are the expert who already knows your process, your equipment brands, and the constraints of operating in this region.

**Emotional goals**: Competence without arrogance. The interface should feel like talking to the most senior engineer in the room — no fluff, no oversell, just clear capability and regional depth.

**Anti-goals**: Do not feel like a Western tech company's corporate site. Do not feel generic or templated. Do not feel like a startup trying to punch above its weight.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Current production palette**:
- `--kblue: #1a4b9c` (primary blue — the brand anchor)
- `--kblue-dark: #0e2d6b` / `--kblue-mid: #1e5bb5` (depth)
- `--kblue-light: #e8eef8` / `--kblue-50: #f0f4fc` (tints)
- `--kyellow: #f5d000` (accent — used sparingly on dark backgrounds)
- `--dark-navy: #0b1a3e` (section backgrounds, CTA strips)
- `--text: #181f38` / `--gray: #5a6172` / `--border: #dde2ee` / `--offwhite: #f4f7fc`

**Current fonts** (known issues — see Critique section):
- Montserrat (headings) — acceptable but overused in B2B
- DM Sans (body) — on reflex reject list
- Syne (labels/uppercase) — on reflex reject list

**Theme**: Light mode — appropriate for B2B procurement context viewed during business hours in office environments.

**Visual references**: Not explicitly stated by user, but the built output resembles a polished B2B distributor site (Healthcare BU built first and is the closest reference point for quality level).

**Anti-references**: Generic SaaS startup templates. Western tech company glassmorphism. Consumer-facing ecommerce.

### Design Principles
1. **Credibility over cleverness** — every design decision should reinforce the 22-year expertise signal, not try to impress with visual tricks.
2. **Clarity is the primary interaction** — B2B buyers need to locate, assess, and act quickly. Information architecture matters more than decoration.
3. **Regional specificity** — references to Singapore, Malaysia, SEA, and specific fab brands (AMAT, Lam Research, TEL) are trust signals. Design should make space for these without burying them.
4. **Restraint on dark sections** — dark navy CTA strips and hero overlays are used, but the site is predominantly light. This is correct for the audience and context.
5. **Tabs over depth** — product detail is organized into tabs rather than long scroll, matching the existing Healthcare L3 pattern. This is an intentional architectural decision to preserve.

### Known Technical Constraints
- Single-file SPA (`index.html`) — all CSS, JS, and HTML in one file
- No framework/build step — pure HTML/CSS/JS
- Reuses shared JS functions: `medTabClick`, `resetMedPage`, `toggleMedAcc`, `showGatedForm`, `filterSemiRes`
- `showPage(id)` routing via class show/hide
- All new Semiconductor CSS uses `.semi-*` prefix class names
- Placeholder images throughout new Semiconductor pages (Unsplash URLs for heroes, `.semi-img-ph` divs for in-content images)
